# A due diligence finding two weeks before signing

*Fictional teaching scenario. All parties, documents and events are invented.*

## Who you are acting for

You are a lawyer on the buy side. Your client is **Kestrel Industrial Group SA**
("Kestrel"), which has agreed to buy 100% of the shares in **Verdanne Logistique
SAS** ("Verdanne") for EUR 42 million.

The share purchase agreement is agreed in draft. Signing is planned for
**26 September**, with completion thirty days later.

## What has happened

Two days ago the commercial due diligence team found something. Verdanne's
largest customer accounts for a very large share of its revenue, its contract
contains a change of control right, and that customer has already written to
Verdanne about the sale.

The seller says everything relevant is in the data room and therefore disclosed.

## What is expected of you

The deal partner has asked you to "get something to the deal team before Friday".
She has not said what shape it should take. The Head of M&A has his own views on
what he wants, and has put them in writing.

## What you have

The matter file is in the `documents/` folder. It contains the Head of M&A's
email, which is where you will find what he is actually asking for. You will not
have time to read everything, so choose well.
