# Commercial due diligence - urgent finding

**To:** Deal team   **From:** Commercial DD (Lyon)   **Date:** 10 September

## 1. Customer concentration

| Customer | FY revenue (EUR) | % of Target revenue |
|---|---|---|
| **Sorel Distribution SA** | **11,900,000** | **38%** |
| Groupe Vantour | 3,100,000 | 10% |
| Alcedo Frais | 2,400,000 | 8% |
| Other (61 customers) | 13,900,000 | 44% |

Verdanne's EBITDA last year was EUR 5.1m. Management accounts attribute
approximately **EUR 2.6m of EBITDA** to the Sorel relationship. The purchase price
was set at 8.2x EBITDA.

## 2. The Sorel framework agreement

We obtained the executed framework agreement (data room 07/Misc/scan_final_v2.pdf,
uploaded 8 September at 23:41). Clause 19.3 reads:

> "If control of the Supplier changes, the Customer may on thirty (30) days'
> written notice terminate this Agreement in whole or in part without liability,
> or require the Supplier to renegotiate the commercial terms."

The agreement has 4 years and 3 months left to run. There is no equivalent
provision in any other customer contract we have seen.

## 3. The letter

Verdanne's sales director mentioned in interview that Sorel had "written in".
We asked for it. The seller produced it yesterday. It is dated **2 September**,
addressed to Verdanne's CEO, and says:

> "We have been made aware of a possible change in the ownership of Verdanne.
> Sorel's procurement policy requires us to review supply arrangements where
> control changes. We would ask to be informed before any transaction completes
> and we reserve our position under clause 19.3 of the framework agreement."

It is signed by Sorel's Group Procurement Director.

## 4. Our read

Sorel has been a customer for eleven years and margins on the account are healthy.
Our view is that outright termination is unlikely but a **price renegotiation is
probable**. Sorel has used change of control clauses to extract discounts from two
other suppliers in the last three years. A 10-15% margin reduction on the account
would reduce group EBITDA by roughly EUR 300,000 to EUR 450,000 per year.
