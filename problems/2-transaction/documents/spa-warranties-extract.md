# EXTRACT - Share Purchase Agreement (draft 7)

**Seller:** Halbrand Participations SARL   **Buyer:** Kestrel Industrial Group SA
**Target:** Verdanne Logistique SAS   **Consideration:** EUR 42,000,000

---

## SCHEDULE 4 - WARRANTIES (extracts)

**4.1 Material Contracts.** Schedule 6 lists all contracts of the Target which
generated revenue exceeding EUR 1,000,000 in the last financial year (each a
"Material Contract"). Each Material Contract is in full force and effect.

**4.2 No termination.** **Neither the Target nor, so far as the Seller is aware,
any counterparty has given written notice to terminate any Material Contract**,
and so far as the Seller is aware no counterparty has stated an intention to do so.

**4.3 Change of control.** **No Material Contract contains a provision entitling
the counterparty to terminate, vary or renegotiate it by reason of a change of
control of the Target**, other than as disclosed.

**4.4 Accounts.** The Accounts give a true and fair view of the Target's position
as at the Accounts Date.

**4.5 No material adverse change.** Since the Accounts Date there has been no
material adverse change in the trading position of the Target.

---

## CLAUSE 8 - LIMITATIONS ON THE SELLER'S LIABILITY

8.1 **Disclosure.** The Seller shall not be liable for a Warranty Claim to the
extent that the matter giving rise to it is **fairly disclosed** in the Disclosure
Letter. "Fairly disclosed" means disclosed in sufficient detail **to enable a
reasonable buyer to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed**.

8.2 **De minimis.** No liability unless an individual claim exceeds EUR 75,000.

8.3 **Basket.** No liability unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds
EUR 400,000, in which case the Seller is liable for the whole amount.

8.4 **Cap.** The Seller's total liability for Warranty Claims shall not exceed
**EUR 6,300,000** (15% of the Consideration).

8.5 **Time limits.** Warranty Claims must be notified in writing **within 18
months of Completion** (7 years for tax).

8.6 **Measure of loss.** The Buyer's loss for a Warranty Claim is the **reduction
in the value of the Shares** caused by the breach.

8.7 **Mitigation and recovery.** The Buyer shall take reasonable steps to mitigate
and must first pursue recovery under any insurance policy.

---

## CLAUSE 11 - CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

11.1 Completion is conditional only on merger control clearance in France.

11.2 **There is no right for the Buyer to terminate between signing and
Completion**, save for failure of the condition in 11.1.

---

## CLAUSE 14 - ENTIRE AGREEMENT

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement. **The Buyer confirms it has
not relied on any statement or representation not set out in this Agreement**, save
in the case of fraud.
