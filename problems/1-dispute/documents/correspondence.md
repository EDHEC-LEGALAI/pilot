# Correspondence file (extracts, in date order)

---

**10 February 2025 - email, Northwind (Delivery Director) to Meridian (IT Director)**

> We are tracking two weeks behind on the data migration workstream. We remain
> confident of hitting 1 March but wanted to flag it early. The delay is largely
> down to the volume of legacy SKU records your team supplied on 20 January,
> which was roughly three times the volume in the statement of work.

---

**28 February 2025 - email, Northwind to Meridian**

> Regretfully we will not achieve Go-Live on 1 March. Our revised date is
> 15 April. We will apply the service credits under clause 4.2 automatically
> from the March invoice.

---

**3 March 2025 - email, Meridian (IT Director) to Northwind**

> This is completely unacceptable. You have known about this for months. Consider
> this formal notice that you are in breach of the agreement. We reserve all our
> rights.

---

**16 April 2025 - email, Northwind to Meridian**

> Go-Live is now targeted for 2 June. The remaining blocker is sign-off on the
> store-level reconciliation reports, which has been with your finance team
> since 24 March.

---

**19 May 2025 - letter, Meridian (General Counsel) to Northwind, sent by email only**

> Our client has suffered severe disruption. Unless the system is live by
> 1 June 2025 we will treat the agreement as terminated and will pursue
> Meridian's losses in full. In the meantime no further invoices will be paid.

---

**2 June 2025 - email, Northwind to Meridian**

> We do not accept that we are in breach. Clause 4.2 provides service credits as
> the agreed remedy for late Go-Live and those have been applied. We note that
> the reconciliation sign-off was received on 21 May. Go-Live will follow within
> six weeks.

---

**14 July 2025 - email, Northwind to Meridian**

> The platform went live this morning. All 214 stores are transacting. We now
> hold EUR 187,500 in unpaid invoices and ask for payment within seven days,
> failing which we will exercise our rights under clause 9.3.

---

**21 July 2025 - internal email, Meridian IT Director to Meridian General Counsel**

> For what it's worth the platform is working well now and the team likes it.
> Replacing it would take us at least nine months and we don't have the budget
> this year. But the board is furious about the season we lost.
