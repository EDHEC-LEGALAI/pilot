# EXTRACT - Software Services Agreement

**Parties:** Northwind Systems Limited (the "Supplier") and Meridian Retail Group SAS (the "Customer")
**Dated:** 12 September 2024
**Term:** three (3) years from the Go-Live Date

---

**4. IMPLEMENTATION**

4.1 The Supplier shall use all reasonable endeavours to achieve Go-Live by the
Target Go-Live Date of 1 March 2025 (the "Target Date").

4.2 If Go-Live has not occurred by the Target Date, the Customer shall be entitled
to service credits calculated at 2% of the Annual Fee for each complete calendar
month of delay, up to a maximum of 10% of the Annual Fee.

4.3 **The service credits in clause 4.2 are the Customer's sole and exclusive
financial remedy for any failure to achieve Go-Live by the Target Date.** For the
avoidance of doubt this clause does not limit the Customer's right to terminate
under clause 15.

---

**9. FEES AND PAYMENT**

9.1 The Annual Fee is EUR 300,000, invoiced quarterly in advance.

9.2 The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.
**The Customer shall not be entitled to withhold or set off any amount** except
where the Supplier has agreed the deduction in writing.

9.3 The Supplier may charge interest on late payment at 8% above base rate and
may suspend the Services on fifteen (15) days' written notice if any undisputed
invoice remains unpaid for sixty (60) days.

---

**14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

14.1 Neither party excludes liability for fraud, death or personal injury, or any
liability which cannot lawfully be excluded.

14.2 Neither party shall be liable for **loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of
anticipated savings, loss of business opportunity or loss of goodwill**, whether
direct or indirect.

14.3 Subject to 14.1, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of this
Agreement shall not exceed **the total Fees paid by the Customer in the twelve
(12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim**.

---

**15. TERMINATION**

15.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written
notice if the other party commits a **material breach** which is **incapable of
remedy**, or which the party in breach has **failed to remedy within thirty (30)
days** of receiving written notice specifying the breach and requiring it to be
remedied.

15.2 On termination the Customer shall pay all Fees accrued up to the date of
termination.

---

**22. NOTICES**

22.1 Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand or
sent by recorded delivery to the address on page 1, marked for the attention of
the Legal Department. **Notice by email is not valid service** for the purposes
of clauses 15 or 22.

---

**24. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

24.1 This Agreement is governed by **French law**.

24.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **Tribunal de commerce
de Paris**.
