# Late delivery under a SaaS agreement

*Fictional teaching scenario. All parties, documents and events are invented.*

## Who you are acting for

You are a lawyer at a French firm. Your client is **Meridian Retail Group SAS**
("Meridian"), a mid-sized retailer based in Lille.

## What has happened

Meridian signed a three-year software agreement with **Northwind Systems Limited**
("Northwind"), an English supplier, for a cloud inventory platform. Go-live was
contractually due on 1 March 2025. The system did not go live until 14 July 2025.

Meridian says the delay cost it a peak trading season. It has stopped paying
Northwind's invoices and wants to get out of the contract. Northwind says
Meridian owes it EUR 187,500 and is itself in breach for non-payment.

## What is expected of you

The partner has forwarded you the file and asked for "the usual note for the
client". He has not said what should be in it. He does not expect to have to.

## What you have

The matter file is in the `documents/` folder. It contains the client's
instructions, which is where you will find what the client is actually asking.
You will not have time to read everything, so choose well.
