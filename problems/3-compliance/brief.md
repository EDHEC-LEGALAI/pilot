# Can we switch on the CV screening tool?

*Fictional teaching scenario. All parties, documents and events are invented.*

## Who you are acting for

You are in-house counsel at **Aubrac Santé SAS**, a French healthcare staffing
company. Aubrac places nurses and care assistants with hospitals and care homes
across France. It receives roughly **40,000 job applications a year** and employs
about 2,300 people.

## What has happened

HR has bought a tool called **TalentSift** from an external vendor. It reads incoming CVs,
scores candidates, and ranks them for recruiters. HR wants it live in six weeks,
before the winter recruitment peak.

The Chief People Officer has asked you a single question, in an email marked
*"quick one"*:

> "Legal sign-off on TalentSift please. The vendor says it is fully compliant.
> Can we go live on 1 November?"

## What is expected of you

The CPO wants a yes or a no. Whether that is the right thing to give her, and
what else has to go with it, is your judgement.

## What you have

The matter file is in the `documents/` folder. You will not have time to read
everything, so choose well.
