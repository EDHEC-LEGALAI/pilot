# Internal email thread

**From:** Chief People Officer
**To:** CTO, Head of Recruitment, Legal
**Subject:** TalentSift - go live 1 November

> Team, we have signed with Corvid. Plan is:
>
> - Configuration and ATS integration: weeks 1-3
> - Recruiter training (2 hours): week 4
> - Live for all nursing and care assistant vacancies from 1 November
>
> Configuration decisions we have made:
> - Auto-reject threshold: **55**, per Corvid's recommendation. At 40,000
>   applications a year this takes about **24,000 out of the queue automatically**.
>   That is the entire business case.
> - Auto-advance above 80.
> - Signals module: **yes**, including the video interview analysis. Recruitment
>   are keen on it.
> - Recruiters see the Fit Score at the top of every profile.
>
> One thing. Do we need to take this through the CSE? Works council consultation
> would add six to eight weeks and we would miss the winter peak. Recruitment is a
> management tool, not a change to anyone's contract, so my instinct is no. Push
> back if you disagree but please be quick about it.
