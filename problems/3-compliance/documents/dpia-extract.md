**1. Description of processing**
Automated screening and ranking of job applications. Personal data processed:
name, contact details, employment history, education, professional registration
number, CV free text, and (Signals module) video interview recording and derived
behavioural indicators.

**2. Volumes**
c. 40,000 applicants per year. Retention 24 months.

|---|---|---|---|
| Model reflects historical hiring bias | Medium | High | Vendor bias test provided |
