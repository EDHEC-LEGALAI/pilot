# Corvid Labs - "TalentSift and the EU AI Act" (vendor one-pager)

> **TalentSift is fully compliant with the EU AI Act.** Our customers can deploy
> with confidence.

**Q: Is TalentSift a high-risk AI system?**
Corvid Labs has assessed TalentSift as a **decision-support tool**. It does not
make hiring decisions; a human always makes the final decision. On that basis we
consider that the high-risk classification does not apply.

**Q: Is it CE marked?**
TalentSift carries the **CE mark**.

**Q: What about bias?**
TalentSift has been **independently bias-tested** by an external consultancy. The
test found no statistically significant disparity in outcomes across gender at a
95% confidence level on the test population.

**Q: What about GDPR?**
Our standard **Data Processing Agreement** covers all processing. Corvid Labs acts
as processor. Our DPA includes the standard contractual clauses and is available
on request.

**Q: Do we need to tell candidates?**
Our templated privacy notice is provided in the onboarding pack. Most clients
add a line to their careers page.

**Q: Do you provide documentation?**
Yes. Customers receive the **TalentSift Technical Overview** (12 pages) and the
**Model Card** (3 pages) under NDA.

**Q: Who is liable if something goes wrong?**
Our standard terms cap Corvid Labs' liability at **12 months of fees** and exclude
liability for the customer's use of outputs.
